# Best Budget Espresso Machine for Beginners

Good espresso at home does not need a thousand dollar machine. A budget espresso machine under 300 dollars can pull a rich shot with decent crema, and this guide will help you choose the right one for your kitchen.

## What to look for in a budget espresso machine

The first thing to check is the pump. A 15 bar pump is standard on budget machines, and a pressurised basket helps beginners because it forgives an uneven grind. The second thing is temperature stability: cheap machines can swing in temperature between shots, so wait a minute between coffees.

A built-in grinder saves counter space and money. If you already own a burr grinder, you can spend more on the machine itself instead.

## Milk frothing on a budget

Budget machines use a panarello steam wand, which mixes air into the milk for you. Keep the wand just below the surface and stop early, because the milk keeps heating after you stop. Cold milk straight from the fridge froths better than room temperature milk.

## How much should you spend?

For a first machine, 150 to 300 dollars is the sweet spot. Below 100 dollars you mostly get steam toys that cannot hold pressure. Above 300 dollars you are paying for features a beginner will not notice for a year. Keep some money aside for fresh beans, because stale beans will never make good crema, whatever machine you buy.
